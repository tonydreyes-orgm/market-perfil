1 · The decision

Two paths. Only one ends with a ready project.

If you do not invest now

  • No environmental licence, no grid non-objection from the transmission utility, no sealed engineering.
  • No definitive concession: no EPC contract is possible, because no contractor signs without a construction permit.
  • The 2030 COD window closes. The next operable window falls in 2033 or later.
  • The node, the land and the grid injection study stay available to another developer.

If you invest the seed capital

  • In 18 months: definitive concession in hand, with all licences and non-objections.
  • The project moves from pre-feasibility to ready‑to‑build — the state every project financier requires.
  • You enter before the construction CAPEX exists, which is where participation gets decided.
  • And you enter with participation: as EPC, with your own EPC, or as equity.
The central point: the seed capital is not an investment in the plant. It is the entry ticket to the table where a US$ 601 million project gets allocated. Nobody can buy that entry later.

The plan uses the life‑weighted average of the whole trajectory, not the best year. Even if the capacity factor stalled at 50 % with a price of 68 USD/MWh, the model still returns an equity IRR of 11.2 %. That is the conservative floor.

The merit order dispatch model takes the plant from 53.7 % in 2030 to 81.2 % in 2055 as variable renewable generation enters the system.

The logic is simple: the less sun and wind there is, the more hours the thermal plant needs. The CCGT does not compete with renewables — it solves the problem renewables create.

7 · Your participation

Four ways in. We are open to negotiate.

The seed capital is identical in all four. What changes is what you receive in return.

A

Turnkey EPC

You provide the seed capital and build the plant, the substation and the line. You capture the full construction margin on a US$ 601 million project.

B

Bring your trusted EPC

You provide the seed capital and we bring your contractor in to execute the works, with your commercial relationship and your fixed‑price guarantee.

C

Project equity

The seed capital is converted into equity at project company level. You share in the US$ 99 MUSD/year dividend and in the exit value. US$ 1.3 M equals 0.73 % of construction equity.

D

Seed capital plus success fee

US$ 1.3 M returned at financial close in 2028, plus a success fee for converting a pre‑feasibility project into a project with a definitive concession.

Combinations also work: seed capital plus construction equity, or seed capital plus a share of the BESS. The structure is defined with you before the first tranche is signed.

11 · What if the assumptions break?

The project does not rely on one optimistic assumption

Fuel is 64 % of LCOE — that is the variable to watch. At 8.00 USD/MMBtu the LCOE drops to 86.09 USD/MWh; at 11.00 it rises to 106.09. Negotiating the gas supply is as important as the CAPEX.
